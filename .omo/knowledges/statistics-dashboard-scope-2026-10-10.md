# Statistics dashboard idea scope

The user proposed a separate statistics interface for Federation and AE networks, with time-series graphs
and dashboard-like visualizations. Recorded in [the idea](../../docs/ideas/statistics-dashboard.md).
Specific metrics, retention, sampling, and implementation are not decided. Inventory, throughput, crafting,
and energy examples are assistant proposals, not verified telemetry coverage. Existing observability code
does not alone establish historical collection. Keep this separate from Jade and automation; no new block
or item was requested. Shared-view double counting and unavailable samples are open design concerns.
