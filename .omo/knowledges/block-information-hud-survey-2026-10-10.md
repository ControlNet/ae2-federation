# Block information HUD reference for NeoForge 1.21.1

Checked 2026-10-10. Jade is the recommended first compatibility target based on representative pack
adoption, not a measured market-share ranking across all packs.

- [Jade](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade) describes itself as the modern Hwyla/Waila
  information HUD. It supports NeoForge 1.21.1; many detailed features require server installation too.
- [ATM10 official mod inventory](https://github.com/AllTheMods/ATM-10/blob/main/config/crash_assistant/modlist.json)
  includes Jade.
- [FTB Evolution official mod list](https://www.feed-the-beast.com/modpacks/125/mods) includes Jade;
  [release 1.42.0](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/ftb-evolution/files/8624826)
  records Jade 1.21.1 NeoForge updates.
- [Enigmatica 10 official mod list](https://github.com/EnigmaticaModpacks/Enigmatica10/blob/master/MODLIST.md)
  includes Jade 1.21.1 NeoForge.
- [The One Probe](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-one-probe/files/all?page=1&pageSize=20&version=1.21)
  remains an alternative with a NeoForge 1.21.1 release. Do not describe it as discontinued.
- [Jade Addons](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade-addons) adds mod integrations to Jade;
  it is not a competing HUD replacement.

No Federation HUD integration, feature idea, or implementation was authorized by this popularity question.
