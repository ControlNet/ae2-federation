# Processor naming brainstorm

## Status

Discussion only. No item rename, registry change, recipe change, or final terminology is approved.
The user now wants to explore a dedicated material -> printed circuit -> processor chain, with printed silicon
at the bottom of final assembly. Ender Dust or a processed derivative is a candidate middle ingredient.
An artist is available for additional 2D materials. The user considers Federation Logic Processor a placeholder
and asks for concepts around decentralization, networking, and connection, informed by AE2 addons.
Earlier assistant suggestions of substrates, Ender Quartz, recipe amounts, or redstone assembly remain proposals.

## Naming evidence

Previously pinned source snapshots are documented in
[the addon acquisition survey](survival-acquisition-ae2-addon-references-2026-10-03.md).
Their local language files were checked again:

- NeoECO: `Superconducting Processor`; Chinese `ECO - SA 超导处理器`.
- AE2 Lightning Tech: `Overload Processor` / `过载处理器`.
- Data Energistics: `Data Processor` / `数据处理器`.
- ExtendedAE: `concurrent_processor` and `concurrent_processor_print` verified in the upstream
  [26.1.2-neoforge recipe](https://github.com/GlodBlock/ExtendedAE/blob/26.1.2-neoforge/src/generated/resources/data/extendedae/recipe/assembler/concurrent_processor.json).
  This reference demonstrates naming, not compatibility with this project's Minecraft version.

## Proposed direction, not a decision

Use coordination among independent networks as the theme; decentralization is a design principle and networking
alone is too broad. Candidate processor names: 协同处理器 (Coordination Processor), 联结处理器 (Interlink Processor),
谐振处理器 (Resonance Processor), 织网处理器 (Mesh Processor), 协议处理器 (Protocol Processor).
Coordination best describes the mod's function; resonance offers a stronger fictional material family and visual
identity, but must be explicitly addon fiction, not a claimed intrinsic property of AE2 Ender Dust.
Potential material-family vocabulary: 谐振复合物 -> 印刷谐振电路 -> 谐振处理器.
No mandatory crystal-growth, new machine, tier progression, remote capability, or gameplay behavior follows from names.
