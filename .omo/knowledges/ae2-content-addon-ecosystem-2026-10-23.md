# AE2 content-addon ecosystem context (2026-10-23)

The user's question about post-2025 ecosystem changes refers to content-heavy addons such as AE2 Lightning Tech, Data Energistics, and Neo ECO, not merely pattern workflow utilities.

- AE2 Lightning Tech's author page describes ME-storable lightning, Overload Crystals, material progression, lightning-powered machines, and Overloaded network infrastructure. Source: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ae2-lightning-tech .
- Data Energistics has a published 1.21.1 1.0.0 file dated 2026-06-06 and alpha 0.2.1 dated 2026-06-05. These are release dates, not proof of original development start. Source: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/data-energistics/files/all . Material, meteorite, and processor recipe findings are recorded in `survival-acquisition-ae2-addon-references-2026-10-03.md`.
- Neo ECO's author explicitly identifies it as an authorized modern port/upgrade of ECO AE Extension, incorporating MMCE structures from Nova Engineering - World. Crafting, storage, and computation become separate multiblocks with three tiers each. Thus a new modern project listing does not mean all its ideas originated after 2025. Sources: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/neo-eco-ae-extension and https://github.com/DancingSnow0517/NeoECOAEExtension .

Interpretation: these projects demonstrate AE2 being used as the foundation for substantial technology progression and large-scale infrastructure, alongside its storage/automation role. The evidence supports a visible content-heavy addon wave on modern versions, but not a measured ecosystem-wide discontinuity precisely in 2025, nor an established AI-coding explanation. Distinguish new original work, modern ports of older pack-specific designs, and continued expansion of existing addons.

The user confirmed that their recollection of "万象构建" refers to **OmniSequence: Transfinite**; this confirms the intended project, not an official Chinese title. Its author describes research progression, the Matter Fabrication Well, Sequence Array, virtual crafting CPUs, and endgame singularity resource production. Release 1.3.0 dated 2026-07-25 is explicitly the first public release under the new name and retains mod ID `molecularmanipulator`; it is not necessarily the project's first-ever release. Sources: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/omnisequence-transfinite and https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/omnisequence-transfinite/files/8504859 . The local compatibility catalog pins 2.0.7, and the Federation guide has a Matter Fabrication Well example.
