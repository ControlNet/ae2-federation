# Federation P2P comparison: 100 projects (2026-10-03)

## Purpose and fixed product contract

Investigate precedents for a new mode in native AE2 P2P that carries Federation cable connectivity. The user already
confirmed bidirectional cable equivalence, all input/output ends in one Federation Domain including output-to-output
reachability, carrier ME network exclusion from automatic Federation membership, native P2P power/channel alignment,
and cross-dimensional support in the feature's first release. Concrete implementation is deferred.

This survey broadens interaction and mechanism references without reopening those decisions. A source's one-way
resource flow, public resource pool, operating cost or loading policy is not a newly approved Federation rule.
Product suggestions are in [the P2P idea](../../docs/ideas/federation-p2p.md); the earlier
[AE2 feasibility investigation](federation-p2p-feasibility-2026-10-02.md) remains the technical starting point.

## Most useful findings

1. **Native extension precedent exists.** Applied Mekanistics adds Chemical P2P; Applied Botanics adds Mana P2P,
   with actual native attunement and tunnel inheritance in the inspected Forge source. This supports studying a
   Federation mode inside AE2's existing P2P family. It does not prove topology or cross-dimensional business
   correctness for Federation. [Chemical P2P](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/applied-mekanistics),
   [Mana P2P source](https://github.com/ramidzkh/Applied-Botanics/blob/dev/1.20.1/forge/src/main/java/appbot/forge/ae2/ManaP2PTunnelPart.java).
2. **Management UX deserves first-class attention.** Better P2P lists, binds and highlights endpoints. Its documented
   overlay can become stale, so saved binding, last-observed location and live connectivity should be distinguished
   in any analogous Federation experience. [Better P2P manual](https://lasm.dev/BetterP2P-Manual/Chapter1-1.html).
3. **Transport and exposed services are separate concepts.** OpenComputers Relay forwards messages without exposing
   components on the other network. Current Refined Storage Relay distinguishes signal forwarding from selective
   component exposure. Both help explain boundaries, but neither replaces Federation's cable-equivalent contract.
   [OC Relay](https://ocdoc.cil.li/block:switch), [RS Relay](https://refinedmods.com/refined-storage/networking/relay.html).
4. **Multiplexing and routing are useful analogies with different semantics.** Ender IO, XNet, LaserIO, Project Red
   and Pipez show typed/color channels and explicit endpoints. Names/colors can clarify the carried connection;
   their routing direction, priority and filters must not make Federation P2P one-way.
5. **A proxy is not necessarily a network bridge.** Entangled and CapabilityProxy expose interfaces; Compact Machines'
   pre-CM7 tunnels map inner/outer faces. Compatibility with arbitrary grid nodes must be investigated separately.
   Portable wireless terminals and boosters are client access, not evidence of fixed-node cable continuity.
6. **Availability needs a readable explanation.** Separate pair identity, carrier availability, destination loading
   and permission status. Cross-dimensional support does not itself authorize forced chunk loading or promise
   unloaded endpoints operate. The future coding agent must qualify complete operations and lifecycle transitions.

These are conclusions and design inferences from the sources below, not instructions to select an implementation.

## Method and counting

The user explicitly requested approximately 100 mods for P2P and approximately 100 for policy, with overlap allowed.
Three parallel investigations and a parent review assembled 118 candidate projects and examined each through both
lenses. Each final catalog selects 100; **93 projects overlap, so the two catalogs cover 107 distinct projects**.
Their conclusions are different evaluations, not a reused claim that every wireless mod also implements policies.

Count separately published mod projects/modules, not blocks, Minecraft versions or loader ports. Thermal, RFTools,
Project Red and Integrated suites contain separately published modules but represent fewer design families than
their project count. Original/successor lineages and simple ports are consolidated where noted. Pipe and Pipez are
different authors/projects; the Cloud Storage entry is AlexModGuy's project. The CF and MR editions of one mod do
not create two entries. KilaGraph and KilaGraphDemo remain explicit user references outside the numbered sample;
they are not counted as popular technology mods to inflate it.

The sample is anchored in widely downloaded technology, storage, transport and automation mods, with clearly
identified scripting/protection tools and a few smaller specialist references. Download figures are approximate
lifetime platform counters observed on 2026-10-03 or returned in cached author-page results; CF = CurseForge,
MR = Modrinth. They establish scale, not unique players, active usage, a ranked top 100 or design quality. No
cross-platform totals are added. Smaller specialists are retained for distinctive relevant behavior, not described
as equally popular as AE2, Create or Mekanism. Applied Botanics is an AE2/Botania automation crossover.

Sources are author project descriptions, official manuals and author source repositories. The linked platform
page is also the source when a row says "author description". Version columns scope the evidence; an available
1.21.1 build does not prove that an older manual or newer development branch applies to it. Moving branches are
dated inspection references, not permanent release guarantees. This is document/source research: **no listed mod
was installed or tested in-game**. A screening result means no qualifying mechanism was established in the inspected
material, not that every release lacks it. Unconfirmed suggestions remain subject to future design; the confirmed
product contract above remains fixed.

## Coverage and relevance labels

- **100 projects**, numbered F001–F100; **92** have an inspected platform counter of approximately one million or more.
- **8 smaller specialists:** Integrated REST, Logistical Automation, Ender-Rift, OpenComputers II, Classic Peripherals, Create: Improve, Create: FluidLogistics, Create: Additional Logistics.
- **D: 4 native AE2 P2P-family references**: the AE2 baseline, Chemical and Mana mode addons, and Better P2P management.
- **A: 92 adjacent mechanisms or boundary/UX references**: bridges, typed resource routes, signals, proxies, client access and availability. They are not native Federation tunnels.
- **S: 4 screening records** with no qualifying transport/endpoint mechanism established. Kept visible rather than represented as positive examples.

## Selection limits and follow-up

The wider candidate pass also inspected these projects, omitted from this particular 100-row sample: Mekanism Generators, Thermal Foundation, Thermal Integration, Thermal Locomotion, Thermal Cultivation, RFTools Builder, RFTools Dimensions, GregTech 6, Iron Chests, Iron Shulker Boxes, Expanded Storage, Project Red: Expansion, Factory Tech, KubeJS, CraftTweaker, ProbeJS, FTB Filter System, In Control!.

Omissions favor references with more relevant mechanisms and clearer platform evidence. Expanded Storage had an
unavailable current author page, so cached release text was not used to count it as a well-established current
reference. GregTech 6 had useful official source but no matched CF/MR popularity count in this pass. Neither is
being declared featureless. Popular material/tool/container modules were not assigned machinery or policy
features belonging to another module in their suite. Smaller included projects are explicitly labeled above.

Future coding should return to the version-pinned AE2 investigation and qualify Federation topology, carrier
isolation, one-to-many/output-to-output behavior, cross-dimensional operations and unload/reload transitions.
This research supplies references, not tested compatibility or an approved topology implementation.
