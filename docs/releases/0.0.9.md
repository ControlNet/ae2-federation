## AE2 Federation 0.0.9 (alpha)

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.216+ · AE2 19.2.9+ · LDLib2 2.2.34+. Install on both client and server.

> **Upgrading from 0.0.8**
> - **Statistics history and pinned statistics items reset once on upgrade.** Statistics now use game time instead
>   of wall-clock time; the old history is not migrated. Network rules, stored items and crafting patterns are not
>   part of this reset.
> - **Development builds:** ME Federation Beam Converter, Creative ME Federation Beam Converter and ME Federation
>   Quantum Cube remain excluded while their artwork is unfinished. These blocks disappear from worlds that had
>   them. Federation operating power stays disabled, as in 0.0.8; the `[power]` configuration section is not read.

### Large networks and performance

- Large federations do less repeated work when networks connect, storage changes or Bridges are removed. Shared
  storage, crafting and power relationships are resolved from configured rules instead of every pair of networks.
- Federation Cables, Routers and Switches perform their connection work when needed, with periodic recovery checks.
  Switch faces can recover a connection when a neighbour update was missed.
- Cable capacity counts and shared energy readings are reused. Networks without crafting CPUs and crafting return
  ledgers with nothing outstanding avoid unnecessary checks.
- Statistics writes are spread across ticks to reduce periodic stalls, with cheaper transfer counting and stock
  sampling.
- Removed the internal domain traversal limit that could invalidate large domains. Cable capacity limits still
  apply; this does not make server performance unlimited.

### Neo ECO crafting

- Neo ECO smart doubling works through Federation projections of ordinary AE2 and Advanced AE pattern providers.
- Neo ECO crafting systems can dispatch batches across networks while Federation tracks and returns their outputs.
  In the recorded 256-craft test, completion fell from over 2400 ticks to 96 ticks, compared with 92 ticks on one
  native network. Results depend on the recipe, machines and server.
- Large Integrated Working Stations can receive batches through projected providers, reducing the number of pushes
  and return-accounting operations.

### Statistics

- Rates and history now use game seconds (20 ticks). Pausing singleplayer or stopping the server does not advance
  the chart; low TPS does not dilute rates with elapsed wall-clock time.
- The statistics screen and the English and Chinese guides use the same time basis.

### Modpacks and addons

- Added targeted AE2 Crystal Science compatibility tests and historical addon-version qualification records.
- Expanded New Age Science and Technology tests with real machine recipes, varied Federation topologies, and
  cancellation, unload and restart checks. Regular regressions and separate checks of newer modpack versions help
  catch compatibility changes; they do not certify every quest or addon version.
