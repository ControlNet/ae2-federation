# Nexus Processor

<ItemImage id="ae2federation:nexus_processor" scale="4" />

The ingredient in every Federation device. It takes two steps: craft a batch of Nexus Cores, then press each core
into a processor in an <ItemLink id="ae2:inscriber" />.

## Nexus Core

Three Redstone Dust over three <ItemLink id="ae2:ender_dust" /> in a crafting table make
<ItemLink id="ae2federation:nexus_core" />, sixteen at a time. An Inscriber grinds Ender Dust from Ender Pearls;
another mod's Ender Pearl dust works as well.

<RecipeFor id="ae2federation:nexus_core" />

## Pressing the processor

In the Inscriber: a Nexus Core in the top slot, Ender Dust in the middle and a <ItemLink id="ae2:printed_silicon" />
in the bottom slot. All three are used up. The core and the silicon also work the other way round.

<RecipeFor id="ae2federation:nexus_processor" />

It can be automated like AE2's own processors: an Inscriber fed from one side puts the core, the dust and the
silicon in their slots by itself.

Used in: <ItemLink id="ae2federation:bridge" />, <ItemLink id="ae2federation:cable" />,
<ItemLink id="ae2federation:pattern_provider" /> and <ItemLink id="ae2federation:processing_endpoint" />.
