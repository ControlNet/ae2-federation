# Items and Blocks

Every item and block AE2 Federation adds, with its faces, its numbers and its recipe. How they work together is
explained under [How Federation Works](../mechanics/index.md).

Sneak and right-click any of these blocks with a wrench to pick it straight up into your inventory, as with AE2's own
blocks.
