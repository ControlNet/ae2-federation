# AE2 Federation

![ME networks linked by Federation Switches and Federation Cable](assets/teaser.png)

AE2 Federation links separate ME networks. Each network keeps its own channels, controllers and crafting CPUs, and
shares with the others only what you switch on: its storage, its pattern providers, its machines or its ME power, each
in one direction.

* [Getting Started](getting-started.md): craft the parts, connect two networks and share storage between them.
* [How Federation Works](mechanics/index.md): domains, rules, power, capacity and the Federation screen.
* [Crafting Across Networks](crafting/index.md): order from another network's pattern providers, or send processing
  patterns to its machines.
* [Items and Blocks](items/index.md): every block, its faces, its numbers and its recipe.
* [Examples](examples/index.md): complete builds, from a shared warehouse to an outpost in the Nether.
* [Federation Automation](automation/index.md): Python programs that watch the domain and act on it, run by a
  computing device.
* [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): what the warnings in the Federation screen mean.
